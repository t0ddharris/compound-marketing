# Campaign Brief, Channel Mix, and KPIs

Use this in Stage 1 to turn the `launch-strategy` output into a brief the user can approve.

## Brief Structure

1. **Campaign name**: short and descriptive
2. **Objective**: the business outcome (pipeline, awareness, adoption)
3. **Target audience**: persona(s) from `/brain/personas.md`
4. **Core message**: the one thing the audience should take away. Must trace to `/brain/positioning-and-messaging.md`
5. **Supporting messages**: 2-3 secondary points
6. **Channels**: which channels, in what order (see Channel Mix)
7. **Assets needed**: deliverables per channel, and the skill that produces each
8. **Timeline**: brief approval, asset creation, review, launch, post-launch analysis
9. **KPIs**: see KPI Framework
10. **Budget**: estimated spend by channel, if any

Before writing the brief, benchmark against past campaigns with the `analytics` skill (`hubspot-campaigns.ts 90d`, `hubspot-emails.ts 30d`) when HubSpot is connected.

## Channel Mix by Campaign Type

| Type | Primary | Secondary | Typical assets |
|------|---------|-----------|----------------|
| Awareness | LinkedIn organic, blog, X | Paid social, community | Thought leadership posts, infographics |
| Demand generation | Gated whitepapers, webinars, email | Paid search, retargeting | Whitepapers, datasheets, comparison guides, landing pages |
| Product launch | Announcement blog, customer email, social push | Paid social, PR, communities | Launch blog, datasheet, demo video, social posts |
| Developer / community | Technical blog, community channels, talks | X threads, GitHub discussions | Tutorials, how-tos, architecture deep dives |

## KPI Framework

Pick KPIs that match the objective. Define the target before launch.

- **Awareness:** impressions and reach, new visitors, social engagement rate, brand mentions
- **Demand generation:** MQLs, gated downloads, email signups, demo requests
- **Conversion:** SQLs, pipeline generated, win-rate influence, CAC
- **Engagement:** email open and click rates, time on page and scroll depth, return visitors
